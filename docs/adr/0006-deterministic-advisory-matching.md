# Make destination matching deterministic, explainable, and advisory

Initial destination eligibility and ranking use explicit, versioned deterministic rules, attributed source evidence, and visible reasons. A responsible human chooses whether to contact or proceed with a destination. No LLM or machine-learning model participates in safety-critical eligibility, destination, capacity/readiness, reservation, acceptance, or dispatch decisions. Initial matching is deterministic; later analytics must not substitute for these decisions. This favors traceability and predictable review in a safety-critical workflow over opaque ranking; local policy must still define capability and capacity thresholds.
