# Keep receiving clinical and operational confirmations distinct

The baseline requires a receiving clinician to confirm clinical suitability and a receiving coordinator to confirm operational readiness as separate attributed facts. A designated receiving representative records facility Acceptance only after both are positive; a later Reservation remains separate. This prevents an operational acceptance from being mistaken for a clinical decision, while allowing a deployment to define an explicit, accountable variation before use.
