# Volume II Design Consistency Review

**Review date:** 2026-10-10 (Phase 6 readiness follow-up)
**Scope:** Semantic consistency of the Volume II Phases 3–7 architecture and specifications against the frozen Phase 0–2 constitution. Documentation-only review; no implementation was performed.  
**Review basis:** Read the permanent `AGENTS.md` policy, constitution, Phase 1 engineering baseline, Phase 2 architecture/specification, ADR register, system context, bounded contexts, data ownership, quality attributes, glossary/context, workflow, and all five Phase 3–7 specifications.

## Result

**PASS for Volume II design/specification after remediation.** The initial review found **0 BLOCKER, 5 MAJOR, 2 MINOR, and 2 DOCUMENTATION** findings. The required cross-phase remediation added **3 MAJOR** findings; a Phase 3 readiness check found one additional grant-provisioning gap and recorded it as V2-13. A Phase 6 readiness follow-up found one additional MAJOR: cross-scope owner reads needed an explicit service identity and revision-at-commit wording exceeded the separate-transaction guarantee. V2-14 records the resolution. The final count is **0 BLOCKER, 10 MAJOR, 2 MINOR, and 2 DOCUMENTATION**, all resolved in the canonical documents linked below. There are **0 unresolved blockers or majors** and the open-question register has **0 items that must be resolved before Volume II implementation**. This is a design review, not implementation acceptance, clinical safety certification, regulatory assessment, or security penetration test.
